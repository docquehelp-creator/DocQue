Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information DocQue (“we”, “us”) collects when you use docque.co.in, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Contact form and emails. If you write to us, we receive your name, email address, whether you are a patient, doctor, clinic or other, and your message.
  • Analytics (only if you accept). If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to see how the site is used: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location. If you decline, analytics does not run.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records basic request data, such as IP address and browser, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

This website does not have accounts or payments, and we do not want medical information through it. Please do not send health details or emergencies through the contact form.

How we use it

To reply to your message, to understand and improve the website, to keep it secure, and to meet legal obligations.

Cookies

We ask for your choice before using analytics cookies. You can change it at any time with “Cookie settings” in the page footer. The site works the same whichever you choose.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use service providers to run the site: Vercel (hosting), Google (analytics and web fonts), and an email or form service to receive your messages. They process data on our behalf. Some of them may process it outside India. Google Fonts are loaded from Google, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as needed to respond and for our records, then delete them. Analytics data is kept according to our analytics settings.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is not intended for children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
