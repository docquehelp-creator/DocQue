About DocQue

Healthcare that is easier to reach.

DocQue is a healthcare technology platform designed to simplify how people discover doctors, connect with healthcare professionals and access medical services.

We aim to bridge the gap between patients and doctors by making healthcare more accessible, convenient and connected, whether someone wants to visit a doctor in person or consult online.

What patients can do
  • Discover doctors and clinics
  • Explore doctor profiles and available information
  • Check ratings and reviews
  • Book appointments at clinics
  • Connect with doctors through online chat consultations
  • Access healthcare more conveniently, without always needing an immediate physical visit
How it works

Five simple steps.

Online consultation through chat

Not every visit needs a trip.

DocQue aims to make connecting with doctors easier through online chat consultations.

  • Discover doctors offering online consultations
  • Start a consultation through chat
  • Explain your health concerns
  • Communicate directly with the doctor
  • Receive medical guidance remotely

Online consultations are not a replacement for emergency medical care, or for physical examinations when required.

Why DocQue?

Technology should make healthcare easier, not more complicated.

Healthcare is an essential part of everyone’s life, but accessing it can be inconvenient. Patients may struggle to find the right doctor, spend considerable time arranging visits or find it hard to connect with healthcare professionals.

DocQue aims to address these challenges by bringing doctor discovery, clinic appointments and online consultations together.

Our vision

A connected healthcare ecosystem.

We want discovering doctors, accessing clinics and communicating with healthcare professionals to become simple and convenient.

We want to make quality healthcare more accessible, particularly in growing towns and underserved communities. The future of healthcare is not just about hospitals and clinics. It is also about making the connection between patients and doctors easier.
